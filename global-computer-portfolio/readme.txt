GLOBAL COMPUTER PORTFOLIO — WORDPRESS THEME
============================================

A responsive personal portfolio for Saidul Islam with a separate Global Computer & Technology business profile page. The business page contains the supplied physical computer services, digital service areas, business owner and business phone. The business logo, personal photo and official registration/address details are editable in WordPress.

INSTALLATION
------------
1. Sign in to WordPress Admin.
2. Go to Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
3. Select global-computer-portfolio.zip and choose Install Now.
4. Choose Activate.
5. On activation, the theme creates a published page at /global-computer-technology/ if a page with that slug does not already exist. The business shortcut in the header and homepage links to this page.

PERSONAL AND BUSINESS DETAILS
-----------------------------
Go to Appearance > Customize.

- Personal Profile: update the name, professional title, home headline, introduction, about text, optional photo, and confirmed personal contact/social links.
- Business Profile: update the business name, owner, optional owner title, phone, business introduction, service lists and optional business logo.
- WordPress Admin > Appearance > Customize > Business Profile also contains fields for the exact trade/business license number, issuing authority, registered address and shop address.

IMPORTANT LEGAL DETAIL NOTE
--------------------------
The original brief did not contain a license number, issuing authority, or address. Those fields therefore start blank and are not displayed to visitors until real details are entered. Do not enter guessed, example, or placeholder legal information. The theme does not invent a business address or claim a license exists.

LOGO AND PHOTO
--------------
Upload the real personal portrait in Personal Profile and the shop's real logo in Business Profile. If no image is uploaded, the theme uses a simple initials/text fallback. The fallback is not an official logo.

PORTFOLIO PROJECTS
------------------
To add a real project, go to Portfolio Projects > Add Portfolio Project. Add a title, a short excerpt, project details, and a featured image. An optional external project URL can be entered in the Project link box. The homepage shows up to three published projects and hides the section when none exist. No example or fabricated projects are included.

NAVIGATION
----------
A built-in About, Expertise and Contact navigation appears until a WordPress menu is assigned. You can assign a custom menu under Appearance > Menus or Appearance > Customize > Menus. The business-logo shortcut remains visible independently of the custom menu.

TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
- Classic PHP theme, no build step and no required plugin.
- Tested target: WordPress 6.0+ and PHP 7.4+.
- Theme assets are local; no external font, tracking, or framework dependency is required.
- The homepage's known business phone links to the business contact page/phone actions; it is not represented as a separate personal number.
